# Legal source verification protocol

1. Define the jurisdiction and date relevant to the user's question. Do not assume that all federal laws apply identically in AJK or GB.
2. Obtain official text from a government gazette, official statute publication or authoritative court repository. Preserve title, section, publication date, amendment history and exact URL.
3. Compare against subsequent amendments, repeal and commencement provisions. If uncertain, keep `status='draft'` and `text_is_verified=0`.
4. Record source reviewer, review date and version. Do not mark an entire portal's content as reviewed merely because its URL is official.
5. Separate verbatim legal text from plain-language explanations. Clearly label conditions, exceptions, geographic scope and whether an interpretation is a court holding or an educational summary.
6. For judgments, record case name, court, citation, date, authoritative URL, precise holding and binding scope. Never invent cases or use a news summary as the primary authority.
7. For procedures, verify the correct authority, document requirements, filing routes, costs, deadlines and appeals. Avoid guessed fees or timelines.
8. When rules differ by province, provide a nationwide overview and individual reviewed local differences. Unknown local differences must be labelled unknown, not absent.
9. Recheck sources on a scheduled basis and after relevant gazette amendments or court decisions. Retire superseded entries without deleting audit evidence.
10. Test high-risk topics (sexual assault, arrests, custody, land eviction, deadlines) with qualified legal reviewers before public access.

## Content status

- `draft`: internal research, never shown as law to the public.
- `verified`: reviewed against primary legal sources; may appear in search and AI retrieval.
- `retired`: outdated, withdrawn or superseded; excluded from public search.

## Privacy

Do not ask users to submit names, CNIC numbers, victim identities, minors' personal information, medical records or confidential case documents into the public chatbot. Provide referral to qualified professionals when case-specific advice is necessary.
