# Legal coverage plan (not yet populated)

The platform aims to cover Pakistan-wide and area-specific law. The following are **research workstreams**, not claims of verified coverage:

- Constitution, fundamental rights, administrative law, information rights.
- Sexual violence, survivor protections, harassment, domestic violence, marriage, inheritance, family law.
- Students, higher education, school and university regulations; teachers and public employment.
- Government officers, public service rules, official powers and administrative accountability.
- Police complaints, FIR, criminal procedure, evidence, arrest, bail, trial, sentencing and appeals.
- Agriculture, land revenue, title, demarcation, irrigation, tenancy, inheritance, possession disputes.
- Labour, wages, occupational safety, pensions, social security, disability, consumer protection.
- Property, rent, business, contracts, licensing, taxation, cybercrime and digital privacy.
- Federal law plus Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan, ICT, GB and AJK, with differences clearly marked.
- International human rights instruments, ratification, reservations and domestic implementation.

Every topic should ultimately include exact verified statutory text, explanation, scope/limitations, verified judgments, procedures, appeal/escalation, and dated primary sources. Do not present a topic as complete unless reviewed for that jurisdiction and effective date.
